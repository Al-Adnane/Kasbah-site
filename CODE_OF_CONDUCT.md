# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Kasbah Guard is built on principles of digital sovereignty, privacy, and
respect. We are committed to providing a welcoming environment for everyone.

## Standards

### Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions
- Focus on what is best for data privacy and user protection
- Accept constructive feedback
- Show empathy towards other community members

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Submitting code designed to exfiltrate or expose user data
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces including GitHub,
forums, chat channels, and any public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to conduct@bekasbah.com. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly.
