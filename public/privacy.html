Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 24, 2026

Kasbah Guard processes everything locally on your device. We never see, collect, or transmit the content you type, paste, or upload into AI tools.

What Kasbah Guard does

Kasbah Guard is a local-first data loss prevention tool. It intercepts actions (send, paste, upload, download, edit, browse) on AI chat platforms and analyzes them for sensitive content — entirely on your machine.

Data we do NOT collect

  • Your prompts, messages, or clipboard content
  • Files you upload or download
  • AI responses or conversations
  • Screenshots, keystrokes, or browsing history outside AI platforms
  • Personal information beyond what you provide during account creation

Data we collect

  • Account data: Email address, hashed password (PBKDF2). Stored in Cloudflare KV. Used for authentication only.
  • Extension telemetry: None. The extension communicates exclusively with your local Guard instance on 127.0.0.1:8788.
  • Crash reports: None currently. If we add crash reporting, it will never include content data.

Zero Data Egress

All risk analysis, decision-making, and audit logging happens on your device. The Guard desktop app runs an HTTP server on localhost (127.0.0.1:8788). No content data is ever sent to our servers or any third party. Your audit trail is stored locally in SQLite and secured with a SHA-256 hash chain.

Browser extension permissions

  • activeTab: To read the text you're about to send to an AI tool — only on supported AI platforms.
  • storage: To persist your extension preferences locally in your browser.
  • host_permissions: To communicate with your local Guard instance and match supported AI platform URLs.

Desktop application

  • Clipboard monitoring: Scans clipboard content for sensitive data. Content is analyzed locally and never transmitted.
  • File watching: Monitors specified directories for sensitive files. Content is analyzed locally.
  • Audit trail: All decisions are logged locally with SHA-256 hash chain integrity. You control deletion.
  • Session data: Stored in macOS Keychain and local JSON file. Never transmitted.

Third-party services

  • Cloudflare: Hosts our website and API worker. Processes only authentication requests.
  • Resend: Sends verification emails during registration.

Data retention

Account data is retained as long as your account is active. Local audit logs are retained indefinitely on your device — you control deletion. We have no access to your local data.

Your rights

You can delete your account at any time. You can delete local audit data by removing the Kasbah Guard application data directory. Under GDPR, CCPA, and equivalent regulations, you have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal data.
