Privacy

We never see what you type, paste, or upload. Everything is analyzed on your own machine.

Last updated: February 26, 2026

🛡 The one thing that matters: Kasbah analyzes content locally, on your device. Your prompts, pastes, files, and clipboard never leave your machine — not to our servers, not to anyone else's.

In plain English

When you're about to send something to an AI tool, Kasbah checks it locally — like a spell checker that runs on your device, not in the cloud. It never uploads your text. The only thing it does externally is authenticate your account (email + hashed password), and only when you log in.

That's it. Everything else is local.

What we never collect

  • The content of your prompts or messages to AI tools
  • Text you paste into any field
  • Files you upload or download
  • Your clipboard
  • Screenshots, keystrokes, or browser history
  • AI responses or conversation history
  • The results of any risk analysis

What we do collect

  • Email address — when you create an account. Stored in Cloudflare KV. Used only for authentication.
  • Hashed password — PBKDF2, we never see the plaintext.
  • Nothing else.

How the extension works

When you're about to send, paste, or upload something, the extension sends that content to a local HTTP server running on your machine at 127.0.0.1:8788. That server (the desktop app) analyzes it and returns a risk score. Nothing is transmitted outside your device during this process.

The extension communicates with one external service: our API at api.bekasbah.com — only for authentication. Not for content analysis.

Your audit trail

Every decision Kasbah makes is logged locally in a SQLite database on your machine, secured with a SHA-256 hash chain for integrity. You own this data entirely. We have no access to it. You can delete it at any time by removing the application data directory.

Browser extension permissions

  • activeTab — to read content you're about to send on supported sites.
  • storage — to save your preferences locally in your browser.
  • host_permissions — to communicate with the local Guard process and to run on AI platform pages.

We request the minimum permissions required. We don't request permissions to read all tabs, your history, or your bookmarks.

Desktop application

  • Clipboard monitoring — optional. Scans clipboard locally. Content is never transmitted.
  • File watching — optional. Monitors specified directories. Local only.
  • Session data — stored in macOS Keychain and a local JSON file. Never transmitted.

Third-party services

  • Cloudflare — hosts our website and API. Processes authentication requests only.
  • Resend — sends verification emails at registration.

Neither service sees your content. Neither service participates in analysis.

Data retention

Account data stays as long as your account is active. Delete your account and it's gone. Local audit logs exist on your machine under your control — we have no copies and no access.

Your rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws, you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data at any time. Email privacy@bekasbah.com.
