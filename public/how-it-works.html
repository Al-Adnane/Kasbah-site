How Kasbah Works

Invisible protection. On your device. No cloud, no exceptions.

Three Simple Steps

1 Watch

Kasbah watches for 6 critical moments: sending emails, pasting into fields, uploading files, editing forms, browsing with secrets in URLs, and downloading files.

2 Analyze

When you try to do any of these actions, Kasbah analyzes the content locally on your device. No data leaves your computer.

3 Decide

Based on the risk level, Kasbah either lets you proceed silently, warns you with a toast, or blocks with a modal. You always have control.

✓ All analysis happens on your device. ✓ No content ever leaves your computer. ✓ No servers see your data. ✓ Fully auditable and transparent.

Three-Tier Protection

Kasbah doesn't interrupt you for everything. It scales its response based on actual risk:

Tier 1

Risk < 40

SILENT — Let It Through

Low-risk content. You paste "hello world" or your team name into ChatGPT — Kasbah says nothing and gets out of your way.

  • Zero interruption
  • Action proceeds instantly
  • All logged for your audit trail

Tier 2

Risk 40-69

WARNING — Quick Heads-Up

Medium-risk content. You paste something that looks like a credential but isn't quite certain. Kasbah shows a light toast: "Heads up: possible API key."

  • Inline toast (not blocking)
  • You see the warning and decide
  • Action proceeds after you click or dismiss

Tier 3

Risk ≥ 70

BLOCK — Stop & Confirm

High-risk content. Credit card, API key, SSN, private key, or passport detected. Kasbah shows a modal: "Blocked. Your sensitive data stayed on your device."

  • Full modal appears
  • Shows what was detected
  • You can "Block" (recommended) or "Proceed Anyway"

What Kasbah Detects

100+ Patterns Across Categories

Payment: Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover)
ID: Social Security Numbers, Passports, Driver Licenses
API Keys: OpenAI, GitHub, AWS, Bearer tokens
Encryption: Private keys (PEM, RSA), SSH keys
Databases: MongoDB, PostgreSQL, MySQL connection strings
Tokens: JWT tokens, OAuth tokens
Passwords: Assignment patterns (password=, api_key:, SECRET_=)
Dangerous: Prompt injection, shell commands (rm -rf, drop table)
Documents: Filenames in 9 languages (passport.jpg, DNI.pdf, etc.)
Environmental: Config files with secrets (.env, secret=, TOKEN=)

The Detection Engine

Kasbah combines multiple techniques to catch real leaks while avoiding false alarms:

  • Shannon Entropy Analysis — Measures randomness (tokens are random, names aren't)🔍
  • Pattern Recognition — 100+ regex patterns for structured data (CC numbers, API keys, etc.)🎯
  • Keyword Density — Detects language patterns (password=, secret:, api_key, etc.)📝
  • Character Analysis — Ratio of special chars, digits, uppercase (credentials look different)🔤
  • Adversarial Resistance — Detects obfuscation (zero-width chars, encoding, unicode tricks)🛡️
  • Risk Scoring — Combines all signals into 0-100 risk score. Tuned to 91/100 accuracy.📊

Privacy & Security

100% Local: The extension analyzes content on your machine using Kasbah Guard, a desktop app running locally on 127.0.0.1:8788. No content is sent anywhere.

Audit Trail: All decisions are logged locally with SHA-256 hash chain integrity. You own your logs. We never see them.

No Tracking: The extension doesn't call home. No telemetry, no crash reporting, no cloud processing.

Open Source: Core detection engine is auditable. You can verify exactly what patterns we're looking for.
