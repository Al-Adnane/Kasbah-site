Real Data Leaks We Prevent

Six ways your secrets leak every day.

Email with Passwords

You email a password or API key, thinking it's internal. The email goes to an external recipient by mistake. The damage spreads in minutes.

Pasting into ChatGPT

You copy customer lists, credit card details, or source code. You paste into ChatGPT for analysis. Your data is now part of their training data forever.

Uploading Documents

You upload a passport, driver's license, or tax return to a cloud service. The filename alone reveals sensitive information. The content is now indexed.

Typing into Forms

You fill out a web form and type a credit card number. The browser caches it. A malicious script intercepts it. A third-party tracker records it.

Browsing with PII

You navigate to URLs with personal information as parameters. The data leaks to the server, the browser history, and any monitoring tools.

Downloading Files

You download files with exposed names: customer_data.csv, employee_ssns.xlsx. The filename alone reveals what's inside to anyone watching.
