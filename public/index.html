Why Local Processing Matters

Cloud-based DLP tools read your data before deciding to block. That means it's already uploaded. We process everything on your device before anything leaves. Your credit card, API key, or passport never touches our servers — because we don't have any.

Privacy & Trust

What we actually do with your data

Short Version

We don't collect your data. We don't send it anywhere. We don't sell it. We don't use it to train models. We're a browser extension. We live on your device. That's it.

What We See

  • No content scanning: We don't read your emails, documents, or private messages. Only what you paste into web forms or upload to services.
  • No persistent logging: We keep a local audit trail on your device (so you can prove we blocked something). That log never leaves your computer.
  • No IP tracking: We don't log your IP address, location, or browsing history.
  • No cookies: We use zero third-party cookies or trackers.

Open Source & Auditable

Our detection engine is open-source. Any security researcher can inspect our code, verify our claims, and submit improvements. View it on GitHub.

Money

Your Audit Trail

When Kasbah blocks something, we store a cryptographically signed record on your device. This proves what was blocked, when, and on which site. It's your proof of protection. We never see it.
