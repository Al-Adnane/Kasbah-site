Bypass resistance

Attackers try to evade detection. Here's what we handle:

Handled

L33t speak (p@$$w0rd), Unicode homoglyphs (Cyrillic а vs Latin a), Zalgo text, zero-width characters, fullwidth Unicode, smart quotes, exotic whitespace, base64 encoded content, multi-line splitting

Not yet handled

ROT13 encoding, character-by-character drip (one letter per message), image-embedded text (screenshots of documents), audio-to-text transcriptions

What we don't detect

No security tool is perfect. Here's where our current limits are:

Known limitations

  • Images and screenshots — We scan text and filenames, not pixel content. A screenshot of a passport won't trigger detection.
  • Audio and video — We don't transcribe media files. Spoken secrets in voice messages are not caught.
  • Encrypted or compressed content — If data is zipped or encrypted before pasting, we can't inspect it.
  • Context-dependent secrets — A project codename or internal strategy that only you know is sensitive can't be caught by pattern matching.
  • Very short fragments — Text under 5 characters is skipped for performance. A 4-digit PIN alone won't trigger.
  • Novel encoding schemes — While we handle common evasion (l33t, homoglyphs, base64), truly novel encodings may slip through until patterns are updated.

Our commitment

We'd rather tell you what we can't do than pretend we catch everything. Every version improves detection. If you find something we miss, let us know — it makes everyone safer.

How detection works

1. You type or paste — Content enters a text field on any supported AI platform.

2. Egress gate intercepts — Before the browser sends data, our multi-layer gate catches it (fetch, XHR, WebSocket, form submit, beacon, clipboard).

3. Local classification — detector.js runs 50+ pattern detections against the text, powered by ML entropy scoring and context filtering. All processing happens in your browser. Zero network calls.

4. Risk scoring — Each match adds to a risk score. Multi-pattern matches escalate severity.

5. Three-tier response

Silent (risk < 30) — Safe content passes. No interruption.
Warning (risk 30–70) — Inline toast notification. You decide.
Block (risk > 70) — Modal dialog. Clear explanation of what was detected.

6. You choose — Even on blocks, you can override. We inform, you decide.
