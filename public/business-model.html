Why Is the Extension Free?

The extension is our trust builder. It proves that our detection engine works — locally, privately, and accurately. When developers or security teams need more (CLI scanning, team policies, compliance reports), they upgrade to paid tools.

This is the same model used by companies like Figma (free personal, paid teams), Slack (free basic, paid enterprise), and 1Password (free trials, paid subscriptions). The free tier creates value and trust. Paid tiers add workflow integration and team features.

What the Free Extension Includes

  • 50+ secret detection patterns (API keys, credentials, SSNs, credit cards, healthcare data)
  • ML-powered scoring with gradient-boosted classifier
  • 19-moat egress gate (intercepts fetch, XHR, WebSocket, forms, and more)
  • 6 encoding decoders (Base64, hex, ROT13, URL, Unicode, HTML entities)
  • 24+ AI platform support (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, etc.)
  • 100% local processing — zero data sent to servers
  • No account required. Works offline. Self-healing hooks.

Telemetry

Anonymous performance telemetry (detection speed, risk scores — never your content) is off by default. You can opt in from the extension settings. We use this data only to improve detection accuracy. See our privacy policy for exact payloads.

Bottom line: We make money by building tools developers and security teams want to pay for. The extension earns your trust. The paid tools earn revenue. Your data stays yours.
