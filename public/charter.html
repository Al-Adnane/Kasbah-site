Digital Citizenship Charter

Version 0.1

Preamble

As autonomous systems become participants in human workflows, they must be governed by the same principles we demand of any entity that exercises power: explicit authority, bounded capability, and instant accountability.

This Charter defines the minimum procedural requirements for any system that acts with or on behalf of human authority in digital environments.

Article 1 — Sovereignty

The human operator retains ultimate authority over all autonomous systems within their domain. No system may acquire, escalate, or retain capability without explicit delegation.

  1. Authority must be bound. Every autonomous session begins in an UNBOUND state. Capability is granted only through an explicit Authority Ceremony.
  2. Delegation must be scoped. Rights are enumerated, not implied. Each capability requires explicit grant.
  3. Reclamation must be instant. The human operator may revoke all delegated rights at any time, without restart, without delay, without negotiation.

Article 2 — Enforcement

Delegation without enforcement is theater. Every delegated right must be checked at runtime, before execution.

  1. Default deny. Any action not explicitly permitted by delegation is denied.
  2. Cryptographic accountability. Every decision is signed, timestamped, and hash-chained. Forgery is computationally infeasible.
  3. Single-use authorization. Each approval is consumed exactly once. Replay is blocked. Escalation requires a new ceremony.

Article 3 — Transparency

Authority exercised in darkness is indistinguishable from abuse.

  1. Every action is logged. The Authority Ledger records all decisions, all delegations, all reclamations.
  2. The ledger is immutable. Hash-chain integrity is verifiable. Tampering is detectable.
  3. The ledger is exportable. The human operator may extract the complete audit trail at any time, in standard formats.

Article 4 — Locality

Authority that depends on remote infrastructure is authority that can be revoked by someone else.

  1. Local-first. All enforcement, all storage, all decision-making happens on the operator's device.
  2. No cloud dependency. The system must function with zero network connectivity.
  3. No telemetry. No data leaves the device unless the operator explicitly delegates that right.

Implementation

Kasbah v1.0 is the reference implementation of this Charter.

  • Authority Ceremony — Explicit binding of human authority with scoped delegation of rights.
  • Authority Engine — HMAC-SHA256 signed single-use tickets with replay protection. Default deny.
  • Authority Ledger — SHA-256 hash-chained audit trail. Tamper-evident. Exportable.
  • Sovereignty Reclamation — Instant revocation of all delegated rights.
  • Browser Extension — Five-verb interception at the point of AI interaction.
