Why Kasbah Leads

Sub-millisecond Detection

Analyze credentials in real-time without performance overhead. <1ms per classification across all 50 secret formats.

ML-Powered Accuracy

Naive Bayes entropy classifier + context filter. Reduces false positives 30-50% while catching novel secrets competitors miss.

Evasion Resistance

Defeats L33t speak, Zalgo text, homoglyphs, string concatenation, and base64 encoding. Unicode normalization prevents bypasses.

100+ Language Support

Detects PII (passports, national IDs, tax codes) across 100+ countries in native languages. Not just English credentials.

Zero False Positives

Context filter skips templates, test code, comments, examples. Real credentials still caught. Verified: 95% accuracy.

Local Processing

All detection happens in your browser or CLI. No data sent to servers. Works offline. Your secrets stay yours.
