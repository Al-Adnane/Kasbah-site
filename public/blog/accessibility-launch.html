What's New

🎤 Voice Feedback — Hear Your Detections

Your extension now speaks in your language. Kasbah Guard uses natural speech synthesis to announce detections in Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Chinese, Portuguese, Russian, or Arabic.

No setup needed—it auto-detects your browser language. Want to override? One click in the accessibility settings.

Who benefits:

  • Blind developers can hear alerts in real-time
  • Developers multitasking can get feedback without looking
  • Non-native English speakers can hear in their preferred language

🔔 Audio Alerts — Quick Feedback Without Words

Not everyone wants voice. Some prefer quick audio cues—short tones that tell you what's happening without saying a word.

5 alert types:

  • Low Risk — 600 Hz, single beep (informational)
  • Medium Risk — 750 Hz, double beep (caution)
  • High Risk — 1000 Hz, triple beep (action needed)
  • Success — 800 Hz, rising tone (task complete)
  • Error — 400 Hz, falling tone (something went wrong)

Who benefits:

  • Deaf users get visual + audio feedback
  • Silent environment users (libraries, offices) get non-intrusive feedback
  • Neurodivergent users appreciate predictable audio

⌨️ Full Keyboard Navigation — No Mouse Required

We removed every mouse dependency. Your entire workflow is keyboard-accessible:

? — Show help & keyboard shortcuts
Alt+D — Toggle detection on/off
Alt+R — Redact selected text
Alt+V — Toggle voice feedback
Esc — Close popup
Tab — Navigate UI elements
Enter/Space — Activate buttons

No keyboard traps. You can always escape. Focus is visible at all times (3px bright outline).

Who benefits:

  • Developers with motor disabilities work at full speed
  • Power users work faster with keyboard shortcuts
  • Everyone benefits from keyboard alternatives

📢 Screen Reader Support — Works With Your Tools

ARIA live regions announce changes as they happen. Compatible with:

  • NVDA (Windows)
  • JAWS (Windows)
  • VoiceOver (Mac/iOS)
  • Narrator (Windows)
  • TalkBack (Android)

Who benefits: Blind developers maintain independence and workflow control.

🎨 System Preferences Support — Your Settings Respected

Your system settings cascade through Kasbah Guard:

  • Dark Mode — Auto-switches to dark colors if your OS prefers it
  • High Contrast — Increases borders and contrast when enabled
  • Reduced Motion — Disables all animations if you prefer it

Who benefits:

  • Low vision users get color customization
  • Neurodivergent users with motion sensitivity aren't affected
  • Your accessibility preferences work everywhere

Technical Foundation

WCAG 2.1 Level AA Certified

We met 14+ criteria from the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines:

  • 1.1.1 Non-text Content
  • 1.3.1 Info and Relationships
  • 1.4.3 Contrast Minimum (4.5:1 text, 3:1 UI)
  • 2.1.1 Keyboard (all features keyboard accessible)
  • 2.1.2 No Keyboard Trap
  • 2.4.3 Focus Order (logical, left-to-right)
  • 2.4.7 Focus Visible (3px bright outline)
  • 2.5.4 Motion Actuation (respects prefers-reduced-motion)
  • 3.3.2 Labels or Instructions (all inputs labeled)
  • 4.1.3 Status Messages (ARIA live announcements)
  • ...and 4 more

How to Get Started

Your extension is already updated. Just:

  1. Click the extension popup (top-right corner)
  2. Look for the ♿ Accessibility section
  3. Enable the features you need:
    • 🌐 Language (auto-detect or manual)
    • 📢 Screen Reader Support
    • 🔔 Audio Alerts
    • 🎤 Voice Feedback (TTS)
ℹ️ Note: If you're using an older version, the update will arrive automatically. If you don't see the accessibility settings, try restarting your browser.

The Philosophy

Accessibility isn't a feature. It's not something you add at the end. It's not a checkbox on a roadmap.

Accessibility is fundamental to good design.

When you start by building for everyone—not just the majority—everything gets better:

  • Better keyboard navigation benefits power users
  • Better color contrast benefits everyone in bright sunlight
  • Better audio feedback benefits people in noisy environments
  • Better voice support benefits non-native speakers
  • Simpler, clearer language benefits everyone

We started building Kasbah Guard with one question: "How would I design this if I couldn't see?"

That question shaped everything. And now everyone benefits.
