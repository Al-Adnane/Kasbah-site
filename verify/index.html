Verification

Local-first enforcement: decisions should happen on-device before data leaves your machine.

Deterministic gates: allow / review / block is driven by explicit rules, not vague promises.

Auditability: decisions are explainable in plain language.

This page states product principles. Do not claim certifications you do not have.

What to expect in beta:

  • Fast guard decisions for paste/upload-style actions
  • Clear user prompts for overrides
  • Minimal telemetry (prefer none)
