# Monetization Model — Antivirus Logic

You charge for control, not detection.

## Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Price | What They Get |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **Commons** (Free) | $0 | SilentAllow + NotifyAllow + NotifyBlock (5/month) |
| **Steward** (Pro) | $12/mo | Unlimited auto-blocks + RequireDecision overrides + History retention + Policy tuning |
| **Team** | $50/mo | Shared audit digest + compliance reports + team-wide policies |

## Key Principles

1. Free users stay (no friction) — they get silent protection
2. Pro users pay for: unlimited blocks + history + policy tuning
3. Teams pay for: visibility (digest + audit + compliance)

## Why This Works

- Silent passes are FREE — no guard cost
- Warning toasts are FREE — no guard cost
- Only hard blocks consume guards
- This aligns billing with actual value delivered
- Free tier gives enough to create the "Oh Shit" moment
- Pro unlocks control for power users
