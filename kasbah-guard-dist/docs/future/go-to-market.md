# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Target Persona (v1)

Knowledge workers who handle data they do NOT own:
- HR (candidate data, employee records)
- Sales (CRM exports, customer data, deal notes)
- Customer support (tickets, PII in logs)
- Analysts (reports with customer data)
- Ops (internal docs, credentials)
- Consultants (client data across orgs)

## The Real Product Definition (One Sentence)

"Kasbah is a background security agent that quietly prevents AI tools
from receiving sensitive data — and only interrupts when a human
decision is required."

## Marketing Pivot

OLD: "Governance for AI Agents"
NEW: "Don't Accidentally Leak Customer Data to ChatGPT"

## Week 1-2: Early Adopters
- Email existing users: "We Fixed the Friction"
- Post on r/privacy, r/chatgpt, Hacker News
- Record demo: "Paste SSN into ChatGPT — watch Kasbah stop it"

## Week 3-4: Content Marketing
- Blog: "I Almost Leaked Customer Data to ChatGPT — Here's How Kasbah Saved Me"
- YouTube: "Kasbah v26 — Invisible AI Protection"
- Twitter: Weekly digest screenshots (anonymized)

## Week 5-8: Team/Enterprise
- "Bring Kasbah to Your Team" landing page
- Shared audit digest for team leads
- Compliance report generator (SOC2, GDPR)

## What NOT to say
- No "quantum" or "cryptographic governance"
- No "enterprise-grade"
- No "6 irreversible verbs" (internal concept, not marketing)
- No technical jargon on landing page

## What TO say
- "Don't be the one who leaked customer data."
- "Kasbah watches your copy/paste so you don't have to."
- "Invisible protection. Proof when you need it."
