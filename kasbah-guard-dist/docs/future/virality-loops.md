# Virality Strategy — Portable Proof + Forced Expansion

Kasbah goes viral when proof is portable and others REQUIRE it.

## The Viral Loop

1. User pastes risky data
2. Kasbah silently protects or blocks once
3. Kasbah generates portable proof
4. Proof is shared externally
5. Recipient must verify → hits Kasbah
6. Recipient creates free account
7. Recipient becomes user
8. Loop repeats

If any step is missing → no virality.

## 5 Viral Mechanisms

### 1. Portable Proof That Others REQUIRE

A) "Kasbah-Protected" Badge (auto-generated footer):
```
This output was generated under Kasbah Risk Controls
Receipt ID: KSB-2026-02-93481
Verify: bekasbah.com/verify
```

B) Receipt-as-Requirement:
- Make it easy for orgs to say: "We only accept AI outputs with a Kasbah receipt."
- Downstream enforcement → reverse onboarding

### 2. The "Oh Shit" Moment

At least once in Week 1, user must think: "I would have absolutely sent that."
- HR pastes candidate CVs → Kasbah flags national ID
- Sales pastes CRM export → Kasbah flags emails + phone + deal notes
- Support pastes logs → Kasbah flags auth tokens

### 3. Blame Shifting (Responsibility Externalization)

Add named decisions:
- "Blocked by Kasbah Policy v1.4"
- "Released under Kasbah Override"

Users forward screenshots saying: "Not me. Kasbah."
This is adoption fuel for risk-averse knowledge workers.

### 4. Zero-Friction Entry + Forced Expansion

Solo: browser extension, no account required, silent most of the time
Expansion: moment a user exports a receipt or shares a proof,
recipient hits: "View full receipt → Create free Kasbah account"
Not paywall — just identity capture.

### 5. Dominate One Persona First

Primary (v1): Knowledge workers who handle data they do NOT own
- HR, Sales, Customer support, Analysts, Ops, Consultants
- High risk, low awareness, constant copy/paste, fear-driven

## What NOT to say in marketing
- Stop messaging to "security"
- Stop talking about crypto/quantum
- Stop assuming users care about policy engines

## What TO say
- "Don't be the one who leaked customer data."
- "Kasbah watches your copy/paste so you don't have to."
- "Proof that you did the right thing."
